# 2024 Annual Business Report

## Executive Summary

This report covers the company's performance in 2024, highlighting key achievements, financial results, and strategic outlook for the coming year. Overall revenue grew by 28% year-over-year, driven by strong product adoption and market expansion into three new regions.

Key highlights:
- Total revenue reached $48.6 million, up 28% YoY
- Customer base expanded to 12,400 active accounts
- Launched 2 major product lines in Q3 and Q4
- Operating margin improved from 14% to 19%

---

## Financial Performance

### Revenue Growth

Annual revenue climbed from $38 million in 2023 to $48.6 million in 2024. The growth was primarily fueled by:

- **SaaS subscriptions**: +35% growth, now 62% of total revenue
- **Professional services**: +18% growth
- **Hardware sales**: -5% decline as market shifts to cloud

### Profitability

- Gross margin: 71% (up from 67%)
- EBITDA: $9.2 million
- Net income: $5.4 million
- Cash and equivalents: $22 million at year-end

### Regional Breakdown

| Region | Revenue | Growth |
|--------|---------|--------|
| North America | $28.1M | +22% |
| Europe | $12.4M | +41% |
| Asia Pacific | $6.8M | +18% |
| Others | $1.3M | +9% |

## Product Highlights

### New Releases in 2024

**Product Alpha (Q3 2024)**
A cloud-native analytics platform targeting mid-market enterprises. Within 6 months of launch it secured 340 paying customers and generated $2.1M in ARR.

**Product Beta (Q4 2024)**
An AI-powered workflow automation tool. Early adoption has been strong with 120 enterprise pilots underway and a conversion rate of 68%.

### Roadmap

- Q1 2025: Mobile app for Product Alpha
- Q2 2025: API marketplace launch
- Q3 2025: Enterprise SSO and compliance certifications
- Q4 2025: Expansion into Latin America

The company grew headcount from 210 to 285 employees in 2024. Key hires included:

Employee satisfaction score: 4.3/5.0. Attrition rate held at 8%, well below the industry average of 15%.
