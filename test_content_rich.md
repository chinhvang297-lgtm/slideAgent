# Aurora 2026 Annual Business Review

## About This Document

This report covers Aurora's full-year 2025 performance, competitive landscape, product strategy, financial results, and the complete roadmap through Q1 2027. It is prepared for the Board of Directors and the Senior Leadership Team ahead of the April 2026 All-Hands.

---

## Section 1: Executive Summary

Aurora delivered a breakout year in 2025. Annual recurring revenue grew 34% year-over-year to $248 million, driven by record enterprise bookings and a 128% net revenue retention rate. Free cash flow turned positive in Q3, two full quarters ahead of the plan we committed to at the start of the year.

### Key Themes

- **Growth with efficiency**: Revenue growth of 34% while operating expenses grew only 22%, demonstrating real operating leverage for the first time.
- **Enterprise momentum**: Enterprise customers now represent 41% of ARR, up from 29% two years ago.
- **Product velocity**: 42 major product releases shipped across four product lines — the most in Aurora's history.
- **People**: We scaled from 310 to 540 employees across 18 countries, with voluntary attrition at a record low of 6.8%.

### What Went Well

Revenue growth exceeded our 28% target by 6 percentage points. Net promoter score reached +61, the highest in the competitive set. The APAC expansion (Singapore and Tokyo offices) exceeded first-year bookings targets by 31%.

### What We Must Fix

The mobile experience continues to trail the market by 18–24 months. Partner channel contribution to new ARR stagnated at 12%, well below our 20% target. Localization coverage lags at 11 languages versus 22 for our closest competitor.

## Section 2: Market and Competitive Landscape

### Total Addressable Market

The integrated workflow platform market is estimated at $48 billion in 2025, growing at 19% annually through 2030. Within that, the enterprise segment — our primary focus — is growing at 24%, driven by the shift to AI-native toolchains and the retirement of legacy point solutions.

### Competitive Dynamics

The market is undergoing rapid consolidation. Seven notable M&A events occurred in the past 12 months, including:

1. **FrameOS acquired by TechCore** ($1.4B) — signals that large infrastructure players want workflow capabilities.
2. **Prism and Lumen merged** — creating a formidable mid-market competitor with $310M combined ARR.
3. **DesignHub raised $200M Series E** — to fund an enterprise push directly into our core segment.

Aurora's top three competitors (FrameOS/TechCore, PrismLumen, DesignHub) collectively control 38% of the enterprise segment. We hold approximately 11% and are taking share in head-to-head deals.

### Buyer Priorities Have Shifted

Based on a survey of 480 enterprise buyers conducted in Q4 2025:

- **#1 priority**: AI-native authoring and review capabilities
- **#2 priority**: Security and data residency controls
- **#3 priority**: Integration with existing data infrastructure
- **#4 priority**: Total cost of ownership
- **#5 priority**: Onboarding speed and time-to-value

Aurora scores in the top two for onboarding speed and integrations, but ranks fourth for AI capabilities — a gap we must close in 2026.

### Where We Win

- **Onboarding**: Median time-to-value of 4.2 days, versus an industry median of 11 days.
- **NPS**: +61, highest in the segment.
- **Data integrations**: Native connectors to the top five data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Databricks, DeltaLake).
- **Customer success**: 24/7 enterprise support with a median first-response time under 4 minutes.

### Where We Lose

- **Mobile**: No dedicated mobile app. Competitors offer full-featured iOS and Android experiences.
- **Partner ecosystem**: 47 listed integrations versus 140+ for DesignHub.
- **AI features**: Copilot v0 (launched Q3 2025) is useful but not yet differentiating.

## Section 5: Product and Engineering

### Aurora Studio 3

Studio 3 is the biggest product release in Aurora's history. The rebuilt workspace unifies planning, authoring, and review into a single canvas. Key design principles:

- **No context switching**: Every phase of the workflow lives in one surface
- **Intent-first**: Users describe what they want; AI handles structure and formatting
- **Real-time collaboration**: Up to 50 simultaneous editors with sub-50ms sync latency

Early-access customers report a 3.1× reduction in round-trips between design and engineering teams. General availability is scheduled for Q2 2026.

### Aurora AI Copilot

Copilot v0 launched in Q3 2025. It assists with drafting, summarizing, and reformatting content. Adoption is solid: 61% of enterprise users have activated Copilot, and activated users show 22% higher weekly active usage.

Copilot v1 (GA Q2 2026) adds:

- Intent-level authoring: describe the goal, Copilot writes and structures the content
- Multi-document synthesis: pull insights from up to 20 source documents
- Review agent: automatically flags inconsistencies, brand guideline violations, and factual gaps
- Meeting-to-document: transcript → structured deliverable in under 60 seconds

### Agents SDK

The Aurora Agents SDK (developer preview Q4 2026) enables partners and enterprise customers to build custom agents on top of Aurora. Early design partners include Salesforce, Workday, and three global system integrators. The SDK exposes:

- Workflow triggers and event subscriptions
- Read/write access to the Aurora content graph
- Human-in-the-loop approval flows
- Sandboxed execution environments with configurable resource limits

### Engineering Velocity

The engineering organization grew from 118 to 204 people. Key process improvements:

- Median pull request review time reduced from 28 hours to 6 hours
- Deployment frequency increased from 2× per week to 12× per week
- Production incident rate declined 44% year-over-year
- Test coverage: 84% line coverage, up from 61%

## Section 8: 2026 Strategy and Priorities

### Strategic Thesis

The enterprise software market is experiencing a once-in-a-decade transition to AI-native toolchains. Companies that establish platform positions with deep AI integration in the next 24 months will be very difficult to displace. Aurora's strategy for 2026 is to win that platform position in the workflow category.

### The Three Bets

**Bet 1 — AI Platform**: Ship Copilot v1 and the Agents SDK to establish Aurora as the AI layer for enterprise workflows. This is the single most important thing we do in 2026.

**Bet 2 — Enterprise Expansion**: Grow enterprise mix from 41% to 55% of ARR. This means larger initial deals, deeper account expansion, and a revamped renewal process.

**Bet 3 — Partner Ecosystem**: Reach 20% of new ARR from partners by end of 2026. The redesigned partner program and the certification platform are the primary levers.

### 2026 Financial Targets

| Metric | 2025 Actual | 2026 Target |
|---|---|---|
| ARR | $248M | $310M |
| ARR Growth | 34% | 25% |
| Gross Margin | 74% | 76% |
| Operating Margin | -6% | +2% |
| New Enterprise Logos | 112 | 165 |
| Net Revenue Retention | 128% | 132% |

### Risks and Mitigations

1. **AI competition from large platforms** (Google, Microsoft, Salesforce entering the workflow space): Mitigation — deepen integrations so Aurora augments rather than competes with platform vendors; accelerate the Agents SDK to make Aurora a layer above the platform.

2. **Talent market tightening for AI/ML engineers**: Mitigation — establish Aurora as a destination employer through research publication, conference presence, and competitive compensation benchmarking.

3. **Macroeconomic softening compressing enterprise budgets**: Mitigation — reframe Aurora as cost-reduction (efficiency platform) rather than cost-addition; build ROI calculators into the sales motion.
